Geospatial Analysis of Taiwan's 2021 Nuclear Power Referendum
台灣2021年核能公投的地理空間分析
QGIS Interactive map Nuclear energy

Built an interactive choropleth map showing township-level voting patterns across Taiwan, then layered in nuclear plant proximity and demographic data to surface who lives closest to the risk — and how they voted. My first attempt at combining geospatial risk assessment with democratic data.

製作互動式分區地圖，呈現台灣各鄉鎮的投票行為，並疊加核電廠距離與人口結構數據，揭示誰住得離風險最近——以及他們如何投票。這是我首次嘗試將地理空間風險評估與民主數據結合。

02
The Big [Censored] Theory
生活大嗦嗦：中國美劇中的敏感詞
Media monitoring Data viz Deep reporting

Tracked censorship patterns in Chinese-dubbed American TV series, cross-referencing dubbed scripts with originals to identify systematic patterns. Combined author interviews with data visualisation to show what gets silenced — and why.

追蹤中國配音美劇中的審查模式，將配音腳本與原版對照，識別系統性規律。結合作者採訪與數據視覺化，呈現什麼被消音——以及為什麼。

Rise of Women's Power in Taiwan's 2024 Elections
台灣2024大選的女力崛起
Election data Gender Trend analysis

Four-cycle longitudinal analysis of female legislative representation, using small multiples to show which districts lead and which stall progress. Part of a ten-keyword series contextualising Taiwan's 2024 general election.

四屆選舉的縱向分析，以小倍數圖表呈現哪些選區推動、哪些阻礙了女性代表性的進展。是2024台灣大選十關鍵字系列的一部分。

Mapping Taiwanese Corporate Environmental Impact in Indonesia
台灣企業在印尼的環境足跡地理調查
QGIS Investigative Cross-border Supply chain

Geospatial investigation cross-referencing Taiwanese corporate registry data with Indonesian environmental incident records by province. Surfaced clusters of manufacturing presence in regions with documented pollution and labour rights violations — a story that existing reporting had not mapped visually.

地理空間調查，將台灣企業登記數據與印尼各省環境事件紀錄交叉比對，找出製造業聚集地與已記錄污染及勞權侵害案例的重疊區域——一個從未被視覺化呈現的跨境故事。

About

Data journalist and visual storyteller based in Taipei. I combine investigative reporting with geospatial analysis and interactive design to tell stories about power, policy, and people across Asia. Fluent in Chinese and English, with experience covering Taiwan's environmental politics, elections, and media landscape. Currently deepening skills in Python, creative coding, and scrollytelling — and interested in roles that bridge rigorous data work with human-centred narrative, particularly at the intersection of climate, governance, and social justice in Asia.

駐台北的資料記者與視覺說書人。結合調查報導、地理空間分析與互動設計，說關於亞洲的權力、政策與人的故事。中英文流利，長期報導台灣環境政治、選舉與媒體生態。目前正深化 Python、創意編程與 scrollytelling 技能，尋找能在亞洲氣候、治理與社會正義議題上結合嚴謹數據分析與人本敘事的工作機會。
